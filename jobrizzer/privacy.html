JobRizzer

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how JobRizzer, the AI job application autofill Chrome extension, processes resume data, trial identifiers, email opt-ins, licenses, payments, and AI requests.

Effective date: May 30, 2026

Resume parsing and standard autofill are designed to run in your browser. We use hashes, local storage, Cloudflare KV license records, Loops email records, and Lemon Squeezy payment records only as described below.

Who We Are

JobRizzer, previously published as AI Autofill Job Applications, helps users fill job applications with profile facts, resume-derived information, and AI assistance.

Contact: expdevmgr@gmail.com

Information We Process

Depending on how you use the extension, we may process:

  • Resume files that you upload to the extension.
  • Resume-derived profile facts, such as work history, education, skills, and reusable application answers.
  • Job application page content needed to detect fields and generate autofill answers.
  • Extension settings, including enabled AI providers and optional user-provided API keys.
  • A generated install ID used to identify one browser installation.
  • Resume hash and email hash values used for trial-quota and anti-abuse identity checks.
  • License keys, entitlement JWTs, plan status, expiration status, and license verification data for Pro users.
  • Email address and name if you opt into product communications.
  • Basic technical and security data, such as request timestamps, rate-limit signals, and webhook event metadata.

Resume Data

Resume parsing is client-side. Your uploaded resume file is stored locally in your browser using extension storage and IndexedDB.

For resume parsing, profile extraction, and standard autofill, your raw resume content does not leave your browser. We may send a one-way hash of your resume to our server to remember free-trial state and reduce abuse if you reinstall.

If you choose a feature that explicitly rewrites, tailors, or analyzes resume text with an AI provider, the text needed for that feature may be sent to the selected AI provider so the feature can work.

AI Processing

If you use the free trial or Pro shared AI mode, prompts may be sent through our Cloudflare Worker to an AI provider, currently Google Gemini API. These prompts may include job form labels, page context, job-related text, and profile facts needed to generate answers.

If you configure your own AI API key, requests may be sent directly from your browser to the provider you enable, such as Google Gemini, OpenAI-compatible APIs, or Groq. Your user-provided API keys are stored locally in your browser and are not intentionally sent to our server.

Email Communications

If you opt into product communications, the extension may extract the email address from your resume header and send it, along with your name when available, to Loops, our email provider.

We use Loops to send product communications and transactional messages. You can unsubscribe from non-transactional emails using the unsubscribe link in those emails.

Trial Quota and Anti-Abuse

To provide a free trial and reduce abuse, we use a generated install ID, resume hash, email hash when available, and rate-limit or security signals. We use hashes instead of raw resume content where possible.

Pro Licenses

For Pro users, we store license and entitlement records in Cloudflare KV. These records may include license key, plan, status, expiration date, customer email, customer name, linked install or device identifiers, and entitlement JWT metadata.

The extension stores entitlement JWTs locally so it can verify Pro access without asking you to paste your license key every time.

Payments, Pricing, and Refunds

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy. We do not store your full payment card details.

Current pricing:

  • Pro Monthly: $19 per month.
  • Pro Lifetime: $99 one-time payment.

We offer a 14-day refund window for Pro purchases. To request a refund, contact expdevmgr@gmail.com with your purchase email and order information. Lemon Squeezy may process the refund and related transaction records.

Service Providers

We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate the extension:

  • Cloudflare for Worker hosting, KV storage, security, and rate limiting.
  • Google Gemini API for shared-key trial or Pro AI processing.
  • Loops for email communications.
  • Lemon Squeezy for checkout, payment processing, subscriptions, refunds, and purchase webhooks.
  • User-selected AI providers if you configure your own API key.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Our use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use extension data only to provide and improve user-facing features, authentication, billing, support, security, and abuse prevention.

Data Retention

Local extension data stays in your browser until you delete it, clear browser storage, or uninstall the extension. Some install identifiers may also be stored in Chrome sync if enabled.

Server-side quota, license, audit, and security records are retained as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, prevent abuse, resolve billing or support issues, comply with legal obligations, and maintain purchase records.

Email communication records remain in Loops until deleted or no longer needed. Payment records remain with Lemon Squeezy according to its legal, tax, and payment-processing obligations.

Your Choices

You can:

  • Delete or edit profile facts in the extension options page.
  • Remove your stored resume from the extension.
  • Disable or uninstall the extension.
  • Remove your own AI API keys from settings.
  • Unsubscribe from product emails.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing expdevmgr@gmail.com.

Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention, tax, payment, or legal reasons.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures such as HTTPS, browser-local storage, server-side secrets, and limited server-side storage. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid storing sensitive information in the extension unless needed for job applications.

Children

The extension is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and may provide notice in the extension or by email.
